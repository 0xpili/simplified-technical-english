# STE approved words

This list gives the approved words of the ASD-STE100 dictionary (Issue 7).
Each line shows one word, its part of speech, and its approved forms.
This list does not give the approved meanings or the approved examples.
The dictionary is the property of ASD. Refer to NOTICE.md.

Format: `WORD (part of speech) [approved forms]`

Parts of speech: v = verb, n = noun, adj = adjective, adv = adverb,
prep = preposition, pron = pronoun, conj = conjunction, art = article.

Rules for this list:
- Use a word only as the part of speech that this list gives.
- Use only the verb forms and the comparative forms that this list gives.
- The plural of an approved countable noun is also approved.
- Each approved word has a narrow approved meaning in the dictionary. If you are not sure, use a more simple word.
- A word that is not in this list is permitted only as a technical name or a technical verb. Refer to `substitutions.md`.
